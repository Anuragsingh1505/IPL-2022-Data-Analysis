# 🏏 IPL 2022 Data Analysis: Team Performance, Player Insights & Match Trends

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an in-depth analysis of the IPL 2022 dataset using Python. The objective is to extract meaningful insights related to team performance, match strategies, player contributions, and venue impact.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze team-wise match wins
- Study toss decision trends
- Compare toss winner vs match winner
- Understand match results (runs vs wickets)
- Evaluate player performances
- Analyze venue distribution

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 📊 Analysis & Insights

### 🔹 Team Performance Analysis
- Gujarat Titans won the highest number of matches (12 wins)
- Rajasthan Royals followed with 10 wins
- Teams winning by wickets were more common → strong chasing strategy

---

### 🔹 Match Strategy Insights
- Most teams chose to field after winning the toss
- Fielding first occurred 59 times vs 15 times for batting
- Toss decision influences strategy but does not guarantee victory

---

### 🔹 Player Performance Analysis
- Kuldeep Yadav won the most Player of the Match awards (4)
- Multiple players showed consistent performance across matches
- Top scorers contributed significantly to match outcomes

---

### 🔹 Bowling Performance
- Yuzvendra Chahal emerged as a top bowler
- Other key performers: Rashid Khan, Jasprit Bumrah, Kuldeep Yadav
- Bowling played a critical role in match results

---

### 🔹 Venue Analysis
- Wankhede Stadium hosted the most matches (21)
- DY Patil Stadium followed with 20 matches
- Venue plays an important role in match conditions and outcomes

---

### 🔹 Advanced Insights
- Some matches were won by very high margins → dominant performances
- Individual brilliance (batting & bowling) significantly impacts results

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Gujarat Titans was the most dominant team in IPL 2022  
- Teams preferred chasing over setting targets  
- Toss decision impacts strategy but not final outcome  
- Kuldeep Yadav was the standout performer  
- Bowlers played a crucial role in match outcomes  
- Wankhede Stadium hosted the majority of matches  
- Both individual brilliance and team performance drive success  

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations

- Teams should prefer chasing based on observed trends  
- Balanced team composition (batting + bowling) is essential  
- Venue conditions should be considered in match strategy
